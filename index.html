About Us

Built For The Jobs
Other Shops Turn Away

SIXPRO Precision Lasers is a CNC fiber laser cutting and precision metal fabrication company built around one idea: remove the size and thickness limits that force Indian manufacturers to split parts, outsource abroad, or settle for a weaker joint.

By commissioning India's first 60KW fiber laser — paired with a 13-metre dual-pallet bed — we cut full-length structural members, heavy plate and ultra-large panels in a single uninterrupted profile. No stitching. No welded splices. No compromise on tolerance.

Around that flagship sits a complete fabrication cell: 30KW and 8KW fiber lasers for high-throughput sheet work, a dedicated pipe & tube laser, and heavy-duty multi-axis CNC bending up to 6120 mm. Raw plate in, finished assembly out — under one roof, on one schedule.

  • Ultra-large-format cutting up to 13 metres in one pass
  • Dual-pallet changers for continuous, zero-idle production
  • Mild steel & stainless up to 80 mm, aluminium up to 60 mm
  • In-house cutting, tube processing and CNC bending

A National First

India's first 60KW fiber laser sits on our floor. That is not a marketing line — it is a capability no other Indian job shop can currently offer.

02

13 Metres, Uninterrupted

Parts up to thirteen metres are cut as one profile. No splicing, no welded joints, no accumulated tolerance error down the length.

03

Dual-Pallet Throughput

Every flat-sheet laser runs a dual-pallet changer. One pallet cuts while the other is loaded, so the beam is almost never idle.

Sheet, section and forming all happen under one roof — one point of accountability and a materially shorter lead time.

Fiber-cut edge quality that goes straight to the welding bay. Less grinding, less rework, lower finished-part cost.
